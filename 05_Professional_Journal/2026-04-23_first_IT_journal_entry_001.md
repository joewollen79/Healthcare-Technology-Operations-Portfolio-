# Journal Entry 001

I have now spent several months learning IT after looking for a career change. I initially chose IT for its remote capabilities.

However, after spending these last months learning and building the Training Center, I have found a deeper interest.

I have always been inherently interested in learning how things work. It is deep within my nature.

It is the reason I started studying Chinese Medicine and Neurology. I wanted to develop a deeper understanding of systems and their inner workings.

It is the reason I became ordained as a Taoist Monk, seeking to develop a deeper understanding of all things.

This has driven me all my life, and it allows my heart and spirit to flourish.

This is what drives me to continue in the Martial Arts and my Monk training.

IT presents a physical component to this same learning and path that I am finding resonates with me.

It is not really the glitz and glamour of Tech, or “What’s new,” or “What is flashy.”

It is:

How does this work?  
How does this assist me, or others, in our lives and growth as human beings?  
How can we work in harmony with Technology and show mutual growth?

This is how I have also approached the use of AI.

Fortunately for me, I came into IT without preconceived notions of “good or bad with AI.” Perhaps it is a blessing in disguise, as I have learned to leverage AI as a valuable tool in my own growth and understanding.

Learning to use it as a study partner, a mentor that I can outgrow, a source of research and information to speed up learning and workflow.

I do not see it as the single source of “Truth” or as something “Evil.”

It is a tool.

Learning to use a tool properly is constructive. Labeling it as anything else is destructive.

It has also forced me, in different ways, to learn on my own.

I, like most people who were just beginning, had preconceived notions of AI that were false.

Initially, I did not understand it was only able to reference so much within its token limits.

I thought it was like “Skynet” and was able to cross-reference amongst conversations, remember everything I ever input or used it for.

Then I found that it would have to infer.

After finding out that it operates from essentially two memory systems (more RAM-like or recent in the conversation, and more NVMe SSD-like in durable or long-term memory), then combines these to make more logical work and outputs, I was able to leverage its abilities to a higher capacity.

Prior to this understanding, I was making many mistakes and false assumptions.

I had several network outages just because I was blindly following AI, and when it was inferring a state that did not exist... well, it was entertaining to see what I could do by following so blindly.

Then I went through points where I thought it was leading me astray, which of course was foolish.

I was a newborn learning to drive.

I learned much during this time and now have refined and continued to optimize AI as a tool in the building of this Training Center.

Just like the Training Center itself, it is to be refined and grown, not unlike a plant or a child.
